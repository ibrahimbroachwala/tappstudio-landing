01 About me

Hi, I'm Ibrahim.
I make the apps.

tappStudio is my one-person studio. I design, build and look after every app on this page, from a family archive to a roadside chai tapri, and the ones that come after them.

So when you leave a review, report a bug or email support, I'm the one reading it, the same person who designed the screen and wrote the code. That's the whole point.

Kadak Chai

Idle tea tycoon · iOS & Android

First game

Pour the first cup at one roadside tapri, then grow it into a chai empire across 28 Indian cities. Hire Ustaads, collect family nuskhe, take the train to your next outlet, and come back to Rupees that kept rolling in while you were away.

  • 8 street stalls
  • 28 cities by train
  • A festival every month
  • Plays offline

Nasab

Family archive · iOS & Android

A living family archive. Build your tree, preserve your family's stories, photos, and voice notes, and invite the people who care to keep it growing with you.

  • Family tree, three layouts
  • Photo & voice memories
  • Shared family spaces
nasab.tappstudio.in ↗

Splitup

Group expenses · iOS & Android

Split. Remind. Settle. Group expenses and bills with friends, added by voice and synced even when you're offline.

  • Voice input
  • Works offline
  • Settle-up flows

My Deen

Daily habits · iOS & Android

A smart Amal tracker for staying consistent with daily and weekly spiritual habits. Namaz, Duas and good deeds, with reminders, streaks and badges.

  • Reminders
  • Streaks
  • Achievement badges

Misbaha

Tasbih counter · iOS & Android

A premium tasbih counter with realistic beads and a tassel that move with every tap, living scenes that follow the prayer times, and a count you can carry on your wrist.

  • Realistic beads
  • Prayer-time scenes
  • Apple Watch

Hunar Circle

Community directory · Web

A free, verified directory of skilled Dawoodi Bohra individuals and their businesses. Search by craft, city and availability, then contact people directly.
