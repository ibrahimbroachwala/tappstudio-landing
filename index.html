Realtime Seat Allocation

This real-time seat allocation software is designed to streamline Masallah seat booking in masjids. With an interactive masjid layout, live seat status, and support for group bookings like families sitting together, it enables users to reserve seats easily on a first-come, first-served basis through a secure login system.
