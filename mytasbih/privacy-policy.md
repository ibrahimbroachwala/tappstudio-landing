# Privacy Policy for Misbaha Tasbih Counter

Last updated: 23 November 2026

**Misbaha Tasbih Counter** (shown as "Misbaha Counter" on your device, and formerly called My Tasbih Counter) is made by tappStudio ("we", "our" or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Misbaha Tasbih Counter app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android (the "App") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Using the App without an account

You can count dhikr, create tasbihs and set reminders without signing in. Your tasbihs are then kept on your device. Signing in is optional and lets you back up and sync your tasbihs across devices.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Account information
- If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address, name (if you share it) and an account ID through Firebase Authentication. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive the relay address instead.

### 2. Your tasbihs
- Tasbih names, dhikr text, target counts, counts, completed rounds, streaks, history and your chosen look (beads, tassel, colour, numerals, theme and scene).
- If you are signed in, these are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) so they can sync to your other devices. If not, they stay on your device.
- Daily reminders are scheduled on your device using local notifications. Their times are kept with your tasbih.

### 3. Purchases (Misbaha Prime)
- Misbaha Prime is a one-time purchase made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your card or payment details.
- We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase and restore it on your devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, the product purchased, the purchase date and store receipt information.

### 4. Usage and diagnostics
- **Analytics (PostHog):** which screens and features are used (for example starting a count, completing a round or opening the Prime screen), plus device type, operating system, app version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address. This is linked to an anonymous ID, and to your account ID if you sign in.
- **Session replay (PostHog):** on release builds, the App may record how screens are used (taps, scrolling and what is shown on screen, including the text of your tasbihs) so we can find and fix problems with the design. Recordings do not include your account password or payment details, which the App never handles.
- **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics):** when the App crashes or hits an error, we receive a report with the device model, operating system, app version and the technical details of the error.

## How We Use Your Information

1. To provide the App, including counting, saving, syncing and reminders.
2. To unlock and restore Misbaha Prime.
3. To understand how the App is used, fix bugs and improve features and design.
4. To respond to your support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, and the App shows no ads.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run parts of the App for us, and only for that purpose:

- **Google Firebase** (authentication, database and crash reports)
- **PostHog** (analytics and session replay)
- **RevenueCat** (purchase validation)
- **Apple and Google** (sign-in, app stores and payments)

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights. These providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States.

## Data Retention

Your account data is kept while your account exists. Analytics, session recordings and crash reports are kept for a limited period by the providers above and then deleted. Purchase records are kept as needed to restore your purchase and meet legal and tax obligations.

## Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account and the tasbihs stored with it at any time from **Settings → Delete Account** in the App. You can also email us to request deletion. Tasbihs kept only on your device are removed when you uninstall the App.

## Data Security

We use the security measures provided by our service providers, including encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
