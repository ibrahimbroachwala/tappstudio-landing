# Privacy Policy for My Tasbih Counter

Effective Date: 7th March, 2026

**My Tasbih Counter** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application, **My Tasbih Counter** (the "App").

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use the App:

### 1. Personal Information
- **Email Address or Account Information:** Used when you sign in with Google or Apple to authenticate your account and provide access to the App.  
  We do not collect or store unnecessary personal information.

### 2. Usage Data
- Information about how you interact with the App, such as tasbih sessions, counters created, targets, and feature usage.  
  This data helps us improve the App and enhance your experience.

### 3. Device Information
- Device type, operating system, and app version.  
  This helps us ensure compatibility and provide technical support.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

1. To provide and maintain the App.
2. To save and sync your tasbih counters and session history.
3. To communicate with you regarding updates or customer support.
4. To analyze usage trends and improve the App.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

However, we may share your data in the following situations:

- **Legal Obligations:** If required by law or to protect our legal rights.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
